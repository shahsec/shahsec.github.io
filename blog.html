5 Signs Your WordPress Site Is Hacked

November 2026

Many site owners find out about a hack weeks too late. Check your site for these warning signs:

  • Strange redirects — your site sends visitors to spam or gambling pages
  • Unknown admin users — accounts in wp-admin that you never created
  • Google warning — "This site may be hacked" in search results, or a red browser warning
  • Sudden slowdowns — the site becomes very slow for no clear reason
  • Weird content — new pages, posts or ads you never published
